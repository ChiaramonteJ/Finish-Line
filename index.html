Planning your project

Common questions.

What affects the cost of concrete or excavation?
Project dimensions, removal needs, soil conditions, drainage, equipment access, reinforcement and finish choices all affect pricing. Request a free estimate based on your property and scope.
Do you work on residential and commercial projects?
Finishline serves homeowners and light commercial clients with concrete and excavation work across West Michigan.
Can you prepare the site before installing concrete?
Yes. Excavation, grading and base preparation can be part of the project scope. Tell us about slopes, standing water, existing concrete and your plans for the space.
What should I send when requesting an estimate?
Start with your city or township, the service you need, approximate dimensions and photos if available. Include your intended use, access concerns and preferred timing.
